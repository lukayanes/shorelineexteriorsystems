Common questions

Before you call

How long does an exterior stucco or EIFS project take?
Schedule depends on square footage, elevation access, system type, and weather. A targeted repair may take a few days; a full ground-up hotel exterior runs months and is sequenced against the general contractor's schedule. We give you a realistic timeline with the estimate rather than an optimistic one.
Do you work on commercial projects, residential, or both?
Both. Our commercial work covers hotels, multi-family developments, healthcare and education facilities, and mixed-use construction. We also take on custom residential exteriors and residential restoration work.
Can you tell me why my stucco is cracking?
Usually, yes. Cracking is a symptom, and the cause is generally movement, substrate issues, or water getting behind the finish through a failed joint or termination. An inspection identifies which, so the repair addresses the cause rather than just the visible crack.
Which manufacturers' systems do you install?
We are experienced applicators of Dryvit, Sto, and Master Wall assemblies, and we install to manufacturer specification so system warranties remain intact. If your architect has specified a different approved system, we will work to that spec.
Do you offer inspections before a purchase or capital project?
Yes. We inspect for owners planning capital work, buyers performing due diligence, and property managers budgeting future maintenance. You receive photographic findings and prioritised recommendations.
What areas do you serve?
We work throughout Florida's Gulf Coast — Sarasota, Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch, Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Largo, and Pinellas Park — and travel further for larger commercial projects.
