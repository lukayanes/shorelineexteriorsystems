Manufactured Stone Veneer

Stone and thin brick, correctly detailed

Manufactured stone veneer gives a building the weight and texture of natural stone at a fraction of the structural load and cost. It is a favourite for entry features, wainscots, column wraps, and full elevations.

The difference between veneer that lasts and veneer that fails is almost always behind the stone: drainage mat, weather-resistive barrier, lath, and correctly detailed flashing at every termination.

  • Drainage mat and weather-resistive barrier
  • Lath and scratch coat preparation
  • Ledgestone, stacked, field stone, and thin brick
  • Blended pallets for natural colour variation
  • Corner, sill, and cap detailing
  • Flashing at every system termination

Scope

Blended, coursed, and custom patterns

We install ledgestone, stacked stone, field stone, and thin brick in running, coursed, and dry-stack patterns, blending across pallets so the finished elevation reads naturally rather than in patches.

Corners, sills, caps, and hearth details are cut and fitted on site to keep joints tight and consistent.
