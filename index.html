About Shoreline

Exterior contractors who work the way the spec is written

We are a full-service residential and commercial exterior contractor and experienced applicators of the industry's leading EIFS, stucco, and finish systems.

Our work spans commercial buildings, multi-family developments, healthcare facilities, educational institutions, and custom homes. On every one of them the same thing decides whether the envelope performs in ten years: whether the barrier, flashing, drainage, and terminations were detailed properly before the finish went on.

  • Manufacturer-approved system assemblies
  • Preconstruction detailing and constructability review
  • Direct coordination with owners, architects, and GCs
  • Annual inspection and warranty support

How we work

Application systems & project approach

The best outcomes start long before anyone mixes material.

Preconstruction services

Our most successful projects begin during preliminary design. We collaborate with owners, architects, and general contractors on detailing, budgeting, system selection, and constructability analysis to ensure long-term performance.

Education & collaboration

We offer Lunch-and-Learns and formal presentations for design teams and construction professionals covering current exterior system technologies, best practices, and installation standards.

Inspections & long-term protection

To protect asset value we provide annual inspections, maintenance guidance, and long-term warranty support for the exterior systems we install.
