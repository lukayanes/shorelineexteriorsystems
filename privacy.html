Privacy Policy

Last updated March 2026.

Overview

Shoreline Exterior Systems (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit a quote request or contact form we collect the details you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, and a description of your project.
  • Usage information. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. This collects standard analytics data such as pages viewed, approximate location, referring site, browser, and device type.

How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide an estimate for the work you have asked about.
  • To contact you about your project by phone, email, or text message where you have asked us to.
  • To understand site usage so we can improve the website.

Text messaging

If you provide a phone number and consent to be contacted, we may send text messages about your enquiry or project. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate the website and manage enquiries, and where required by law.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses cookies for analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Opting out of Google Analytics is possible using Google's browser add-on.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete the information you have provided by contacting us at shorelineexteriorsystems@gmail.com or (941) 265-1028.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page is the current one.
